  • The winner is the candidate with the most delegates in that contest. Tied candidates each get the full points.
  • Points follow the calendar order. If the schedule changes, each slot keeps its value.

Major endorsements · +2 each

A candidate earns 2 points for each endorsement from someone on the list below. The group sets the list unanimously before the draft, and it can include people or organizations. There's no cap.

    • An endorser who is also a candidate counts. For example, a candidate who drops out and endorses a rival.
    • If an endorser switches, the first candidate loses the 2 points and the new candidate gains them.

    General

    • A candidate who withdraws scores nothing after that. Points already earned stay, except the −100 penalty, which still applies.
    • Points earned by undrafted candidates go to no one.
